# Wiki-agent: an interactive agent-seat that keeps the project wiki, `docs/nedsnodes-wiki/`, current

The wiki-agent is a planned agent-seat that is answerable for keeping the pages of the project wiki, `docs/nedsnodes-wiki/`, true. It runs the daily wiki check that GHI [Regular maintenance: the list of maintenance jobs, how often each runs, and how each reaches the user](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/issues/1058) plans, acts on requests from the user and from other agent-seats, and changes pages only through its own pull requests. This GHI-MD holds the three parts of its design that the user has decided, the rule that holds until it exists, and, under Deferred-decisions, the questions those parts leave open.

GHI [Hierarchical, cross-linked wiki, and a wiki-agent to build it and keep it current](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/issues/2709) asks, in its Next action, for "the design of the wiki-agent and of the wiki's new structure as a GHI-MD of its own". This GHI-MD holds the wiki-agent's part. The wiki's new structure is not designed here: the user approved its first step on 2026-11-20, in item 2 of overall-cleanup's approval-walk overall-cleanup-wiki-structure-and-wiki-agent-questions-2026-11-20, cited below, as one pull request that puts the wiki pages in five folders by subject, shortens their names and adds a home page; GHI 2709 holds the rest of the restructuring.

## Why

Wiki pages drift out of date when no one is answerable for keeping them true, and an agent that acts on a stale sentence acts on a false description; GHI 1058's section "Why" gives a case. The user's words of 2026-10-16, quoted in GHI 2709's section "The user's direction", ask for "a wiki-agent to do all that and keep it reasonably up to date"; his remark of 2026-11-17 and his answer to item 4, both cited below, make it an agent-seat. GHI 1058 plans a daily wiki check as one of its maintenance-jobs, under the rule that allows only obvious-error-fixes, while on 2026-11-19 the user gave overall-cleanup a wider rule for fixing wiki pages. Two agents fixing the same pages under two rules would treat the same stale sentence differently depending on which of them found it, so one agent does both: the daily check and the work asked of it.

Part 1. The wiki-agent is an interactive agent-seat: the user and other agent-seats talk to it in its agent-session, as with any agent-seat. It runs GHI 1058's daily wiki check, the second job in GHI 1058's list, which GHI 1058's section "What the second job does" describes: once a day, the wiki pages that name a repository file changed on main since the page was last checked, the pages under `docs/nedsnodes-wiki/queue/` excepted, are read; what may be fixed is fixed; and the rest goes to the user through the overseer agent-session that GHI 1058's section "How the maintenance jobs run each night, and how often (decided)" describes. It also acts on requests from the user or from another agent-seat.

Part 2. It changes wiki pages only through its own pull requests, which merge-lane-2 reviews and merges into main like any other pull request.

Part 3. It follows the rule the user approved on 2026-11-19 at 22:51Z, answering "a" to item 3.2 of overall-cleanup's approval-walk overall-cleanup-first-survey-findings-and-cleanup-rules-2026-11-19. The rule, as item 3.1 stated it, where "I" is overall-cleanup and "you" the user: "a wiki page is out of date when a sentence in it contradicts what the code on main, a glossary, a merged design, the Mac, ned-box or GitHub shows. I fix such a sentence in a pull request, citing in its description the file or command output that shows it wrong. This includes an instruction that reality has made false. Deleting a page, merging pages, and reorganizing the wiki still come to you in an approval-walk". So the wiki-agent fixes, without asking the user, a sentence that contradicts what one of those six sources shows, and brings him each deletion of a page, combination of pages or reorganization of the wiki that he has not already approved. In item 4's words, "This replaces the daily check's narrower rule, obvious errors only": GHI 1058's second job fixes only obvious-errors, as `docs/nedsnodes-wiki/nedsnodes-glossary.md` defines one, and sends the user every other finding. The obvious-error-fix that `CLAUDE.md` lets every agent make, such as a spelling mistake, is not taken away.

Until the wiki-agent exists, overall-cleanup fixes wiki pages under the rule of Part 3. Item 4 said so in overall-cleanup's words: "Until the wiki-agent exists, I, overall-cleanup, fix pages under that rule." How overall-cleanup gets such a fix past the instruction-file guard is open question 5 below. GHI 1058's daily wiki check is not built yet.

The wiki-agent is not the wiki-guide-agent of GHI [Two more guide-agents: wiki-guide-agent for the wiki, and agent-instructions-and-hooks-guide-agent for how the project instructs and controls its agents](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/issues/2266): wiki-guide-agent is a guide-agent, which answers other agents' questions about the wiki and changes nothing.

The wiki-agent runs on ned-box: the /start-agent-seat skill starts agent-seats there, and GHI 1058's section "How a daily job reads the Mac (decided)" runs every daily job there.

## Steps that need judgment

The judgment in the wiki-agent's wiki work is: whether a sentence on a page contradicts what one of Part 3's six sources shows, and what the fixed sentence says; which kind of change a change is, a sentence fix it may make or one that goes to the user; carrying out a deletion, combination or reorganization the user approved; and what to do with a request. Besides these, it does the judgment work every agent-seat does, such as talking with the user, answering merge-lane-2's review in a fix-round, and writing its session-handoff. Steps that need no judgment, such as choosing the day's pages and recording which pages were checked, are code, as `CLAUDE.md`'s code-prompt-code rule says and as GHI 1058's section "How the maintenance jobs run each night, and how often (decided)" has each maintenance-job's program do. For the daily wiki check, Part 1 gives the wiki-agent the judgment for which GHI 1058's job program would otherwise start a model of its own; how the program hands the wiki-agent the pages is open question 1.

## Deferred-decisions

### Open questions for the user

The three parts leave these open; none is decided here.

1. How GHI 1058's scheduled program hands the day's pages to the wiki-agent, and what happens when the wiki-agent's agent-session is not running, is busy with a request, or is waiting on the user when the pages are handed over.
2. What the wiki-agent does with what goes to the user while GHI 1058's overseer agent-session is not built; GHI 1058's Next action builds it first.
3. Which files under `docs/nedsnodes-wiki/` the wiki-agent keeps true: whether its charge includes the project glossary, which other agents change with the user's approval; the nn-architecture-overview pages, which /overview-write checks and GHI 1058's twenty-fourth job refreshes; and the proposed-improvements-or-changes-files beside them, which record proposals and so disagree with what they describe by design.
4. Where Part 3's rule stops: what the wiki-agent does when its six sources disagree with one another, such as a merged design that describes a plan not yet built while the code on main shows today's behavior; whether a sentence that states a rule which a program breaks, or a user-ruling, is "an instruction that reality has made false" or goes to the user; and what it does when a sentence is shown wrong but more than one replacement fits, or when it cannot check a sentence because a machine or GitHub cannot be reached, both of which GHI 1058's second job sends to the user.
5. How the instruction-file guard, `.claude/guard-hooks/instruction-file-guard.py`, which protects the wiki, and `CLAUDE.md`'s sentence that lets an agent make only an obvious-error-fix in a protected file without asking, let the wiki-agent, and overall-cleanup until the wiki-agent exists, make a fix under Part 3 that is not an obvious-error-fix, without the user's approval for each.
6. Whether the rule of Part 3 also replaces, for other agents that change the wiki, `CLAUDE.md`'s rule that allows only obvious-error-fixes without asking.
7. Which of Part 3's limits code checks rather than the wiki-agent's judgment alone, such as a check that refuses a wiki pull request that deletes, renames or adds a page without citing the user's approval, and a check that refuses a fix whose description cites no file or command output.
8. How the wiki-agent learns that a page is stale when the change is on the Mac, ned-box or GitHub, with no commit on main, given that the daily check reads only pages whose named repository files changed, for example whether a program checks every path, flag and section link named on any page each day; and how it reads the Mac, which ned-box reaches only through the read-only helpers of GHI 1058's section "How a daily job reads the Mac (decided)".
9. How a request reaches the wiki-agent: by a message from an agent-seat, by the user in its agent-session, or also by a filed GHI; how it handles a request for a change that is neither a sentence fix nor a deletion, combination or reorganization; and when a request counts as done and how the requester is told.
10. Whether adding a new page, adding new text to a page, or deleting a paragraph or section within a page is a change the wiki-agent makes without asking, like a sentence fix, or one it brings to the user, like a deletion.
11. How the wiki-agent groups its changes into pull requests: one pull request for each day's check, as GHI 1058's second job plans, or one per topic, as `CLAUDE.md`'s rule that each pull request is atomic may require; and how changes from requests are grouped.
12. How a deletion, combination or reorganization that comes from a request, rather than from the daily check, reaches the user: as a question queued for GHI 1058's overseer agent-session, as Part 1 has the daily check's findings go, or in an approval-walk of the wiki-agent's own.
13. Who carries out the first step of the wiki's new structure that the user approved in item 2, the wiki-agent or another agent-seat before the wiki-agent starts.
14. How the wiki-agent avoids competing changes to the same page with the other maintenance-jobs that change wiki pages, such as GHI 1058's fifth job, which applies its cold-read-fast-read findings to documents including wiki pages, and its eighteenth job, which corrects the glossary.
15. When the wiki-agent is started, what "until the wiki-agent exists" ends with, and what overall-cleanup hands over to it, such as its open wiki pull requests and the wiki items of its approval-walks.

### Cases left to the build

- How the record of each page's last check follows a page that is renamed or moved, as the first step of the new structure renames every page.
